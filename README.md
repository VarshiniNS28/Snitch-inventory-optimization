# SNITCH – Inventory Liquidation Optimization Engine

## Overview

SNITCH is a business analysis and workflow optimization case study focused on improving inventory liquidation for a fashion e-commerce platform through inventory-driven product recommendations.

The project addresses operational challenges related to slow-moving inventory, excess stock accumulation, low product visibility, and inefficient liquidation workflows.

Instead of traditional behavior-based recommendations, this solution prioritizes inventory and operational metrics to improve sell-through performance and inventory turnover.

---

# Business Problem

Fashion e-commerce platforms often struggle with:

- High inventory holding periods
- Slow-moving and aging inventory
- Low visibility for discounted products
- Revenue loss from unsold stock
- Inefficient liquidation prioritization

The absence of a structured inventory-focused recommendation workflow can reduce conversion opportunities and operational efficiency.

---

# Proposed Solution

A dedicated inventory liquidation recommendation engine that:

- Prioritizes slow-moving inventory
- Improves visibility of discounted products
- Enables admin-driven prioritization
- Supports dynamic ranking workflows
- Integrates promotional offers and discounts
- Improves sell-through performance

---

# Business Objectives

- Increase sell-through rate of aging inventory
- Reduce inventory holding duration
- Improve recommendation conversion rates
- Increase visibility of discounted inventory
- Improve operational inventory optimization workflows
- Maximize revenue recovery from excess stock
